## First video — the intro/devlog

**Working title:** "I Made a Cozy Fishing Game You Can Play Free in Your Browser"
**Length:** 3–5 minutes. **Format:** maker on mic (voiceover is fine) over gameplay capture, face-cam optional.

**Why this video:** It carries all three hooks at once — handmade solo project, free/in-browser, multiplayer warmth — and gives cozy-game viewers a person to follow, not just a game. It's also the link the press email and social launch posts point at.

**Structure (draft outline):**
1. **0:00–0:20 — Hook over best footage** (ducks following in a line or a catch-card reveal): "I made a cozy fishing game. You can play it right now, free, in your browser — no download. Let me show you."
2. **0:20–1:00 — The game in 40 seconds:** five lakes, Kettle Pond hub, real fish from real lakes, journal/badges/aquarium/cabin. Beauty shots per lake, 5–7s each.
3. **1:00–2:00 — Fishing with friends:** shared lakes, booking a camp with a code, friends joining, wave/dance, chat. Clip from shot-list Session C.
4. **2:00–2:45 — The details that sell cozy:** a pet at every lake (run the pet montage), campfire jam, fish rise at the top of the hour, catch cards with ribbons.
5. **2:45–3:30 — The handmade story:** pixel art by hand, synthesised sounds, frequent story-written updates (show a version note), "I'm in the game as Andre — come say hi."
6. **3:30–end — Call to action:** play free at castandcanoe.com (link in description + pinned comment), what to do first (Andre's list at Kettle Pond), subscribe for devlogs + new lakes.

**Thumbnail brief:** Canoe at sunset (match the Andre brand art) or ducks-in-a-line still; 3 words max text: "FREE FISHING GAME" or "PLAY IN BROWSER". Test both via vidIQ thumbnail input before filming ends.

## Next 4 videos (in order)

1. **"Every Pet in My Fishing Game (and How to Befriend Them)"** (4–6 min) — the pets tour as a guide: where each lives, what treat they want, the badge. Evergreen search value + the most shareable content in the game.
2. **"Five Lakes, Five Different Games" — a tour of every lake** (5–7 min) — one chapter per lake: fish, locals, secrets teased (not spoiled), best moment. Doubles as the press-kit video.
3. **Devlog: "How the Top-of-the-Hour Fish Rise Works (and Why)"** (4–6 min) — one mechanic, designed on screen: design intent, implementation peek, footage of a full rise at Kettle Pond. First pure-devlog to test the dev audience.
4. **"I Booked a Lake for My Friends" — a camps session video** (5–8 min) — real session: booking, code, friends arriving, fishing together, campfire jam closer. The multiplayer proof video; needs friends/test players, schedule accordingly.

Cadence after launch: aim 2 long videos/month + Shorts cut from the TikTok set (see `../SOCIALS/short-form-scripts.md`).

## vidIQ research to run BEFORE filming video 1

Do this in Phase 1 prep so the first video is aimed, not guessed:

1. **Keyword research** (vidIQ keyword tool) — score and record search volume/competition for: "cozy fishing game", "fishing game", "browser games", "free browser games", "cozy games", "pixel fishing game", "multiplayer fishing game", "games to play with friends online free". Pick 1 primary + 2 secondary keywords for the title/description/tags.
2. **Comparable channels** — identify 5–8 cozy/indie-dev channels of similar or next-tier size (devloggers and cozy-game reviewers); record: their best-performing video topics, average length, title patterns, thumbnail style, posting cadence.
3. **Outlier videos** — find 3–5 outlier videos in the cozy-fishing/cozy-devlog niche; note the hook in the first 30 seconds of each.
4. **Title/thumbnail check** — run the working title + 2 alternates and both thumbnail concepts through vidIQ's title/thumbnail tools; keep scores in this file's repo notes.
5. **TikTok cross-check** — vidIQ standout-video research on TikTok for "cozy game"/"fishing game" hooks, to confirm the Shorts order in the socials scripts.

Deliverable: a one-page research summary appended to this repo before filming. Brooke runs the research; Canoe picks the final title.
